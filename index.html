How this works

The job list is refreshed by a daily search of LG Jobs (where Calderdale, Kirklees and Bradford councils advertise), council and trust listings, Tes and other job boards, and this page reloads it every time you open it. Always check the advert before applying, since schools can close a vacancy early.

Distances are straight-line from the centre of your postcode area. Drive times are estimates for West Yorkshire roads (about 1.35× the straight-line distance at an average 22 mph). Use the "Directions" link for an exact time. Postcode lookup works offline for Calderdale, Kirklees and nearby Bradford, Wakefield and Rochdale areas. HX4 8LG is pinned to its exact spot, while other postcodes use their district centre.
